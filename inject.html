Classic Chocolate Cake Recipe

Ingredients: 2 cups flour, 2 cups sugar, 3/4 cup cocoa powder, 2 eggs, 1 cup milk, vegetable oil, vanilla extract, boiling water.

Instructions: Preheat oven to 350F. Mix dry ingredients. Add eggs, milk, oil, vanilla. Beat 2 minutes. Stir in boiling water. Bake 30-35 minutes.
